01 · HOLD $50

you're in the crew

Any wallet holding $50 or more of $HEIST is crew. Your raccoon and codename come from your address, so they're yours for good.

02 · 85% OF FEES

the vault fills

85% of $HEIST's creator fees go to the vault wallet. It's public: the balance above is read from chain.

03 · EVERY 6H

the job

At 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC the crew tries the vault. Odds are 20% plus 1% per 10 buys in the last 6 hours, up to 80%. A win splits the vault equally across the crew. A miss rolls it to the next job.

04 · CAUGHT

sell and sit out

Under $50 when the job starts and your raccoon sits that job out. Get back above $50 and you're back on the next one.
